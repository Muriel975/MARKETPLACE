Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, then click "Order on WhatsApp." Your message is already written.

What payment methods are available?

Mobile Money (MTN, Airtel) or cash on delivery.

What is the delivery time?

1 to 3 days in Kampala. The seller confirms the delivery time on WhatsApp.

How do I become a salesperson?

Fill out the form on the "Become a Seller" page. We'll get back to you within 48 hours.

Can I return an item?

Yes, within 48 hours if the item is new and in its original packaging.
